Academic Profile

Professor and researcher in medical informatics, artificial intelligence in medicine, biomedical education, and biophysics.

My academic activity has developed at the intersection of medicine, informatics, education, and biomedical modeling, with a particular focus on the development of medical informatics as an academic discipline and on the responsible integration of artificial intelligence into medical education and healthcare.

My work includes academic leadership, curriculum development, interdisciplinary research, international collaboration, and contributions to the historical development of medical informatics in Romania and Europe.
